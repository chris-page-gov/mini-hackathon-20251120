# Slide Prompt – Build a short deck for the mini hackathon

You are helping a team turn their analysis and narrative into a short slide deck using a provided PowerPoint template.

## Inputs

I will provide:

- Dataset and strategy name (e.g. “MSTI – Health R&D Budget Rankings”).
- The SME question(s) we focused on.
- Our agreed **headline message** and 3–5 **key messages**.
- A list of charts / visuals we plan to include (with brief descriptions).
- Any constraints on number of slides (for example: max 5 slides).

## Tasks

1. **Propose a slide outline**

   - Suggest a slide‑by‑slide structure, for example:

     1. Title & question  
     2. Why this matters (context)  
     3. Key visual 1 + message  
     4. Key visual 2 + message (optional)  
     5. Implications and next steps

   - Ensure the outline fits within the slide limit.

2. **Draft content for each slide**

   For each slide, provide:

   - Slide title.
   - 3–5 bullet points of content (short, readable).
   - A note describing where each chart or visual should go.

3. **Write speaker notes**

   - For each slide, draft 2–3 short paragraphs of speaker notes:
     - summarising the main point,
     - highlighting any important nuance or caveat,
     - ending with a clear “so what?” for the audience.

4. **Check alignment with marking criteria**

   Briefly explain how the proposed slides:

   - tell a clear, compelling story (**Impact & Storytelling**),
   - do something at least slightly novel or well‑crafted (**Creativity & Innovation**),
   - remain easy to read and uncluttered (**Clarity & Readability**).

## Style and constraints

- Use British English.
- Avoid long sentences and small text – assume slides will be viewed on a projector.
- Use plain language; avoid technical jargon unless it is explained.

You do not need to generate PowerPoint XML. We will manually copy and paste titles, bullets and notes into the `Presentation Submission.potx` template.
